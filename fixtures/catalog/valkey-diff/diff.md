## Argument differences

Same command, different shape. Offering the wrong option produces a syntax error.

### `BGSAVE`

- Redis:  `[SCHEDULE]`
- Valkey: `[SCHEDULE | CANCEL]`
- Valkey-only keywords: `CANCEL`

### `BITCOUNT`

- Redis:  `<key:key> [<start:count> <end:count> [BYTE | BIT]]`
- Valkey: `<key:key> [<start:count> [<end:count> [BYTE | BIT]]]`

### `CLIENT|KILL`

- Redis:  `<old-format> | [ID <client-id:count>] | [TYPE NORMAL | MASTER | SLAVE | REPLICA | PUBSUB] | [USER <username>] | [ADDR <addr>] | [LADDR <laddr>] | [SKIPME YES | NO] | [MAXAGE <maxage:count>] ...`
- Valkey: `<old-format> | [ID <client-id:count> ...] | [TYPE NORMAL | MASTER | PRIMARY | SLAVE | REPLICA | PUBSUB] | [USER <username>] | [ADDR <addr>] | [LADDR <laddr>] | [SKIPME YES | NO] | [MAXAGE <maxage:count>] ...`
- Valkey-only keywords: `PRIMARY`

### `CLIENT|LIST`

- Redis:  `[TYPE NORMAL | MASTER | REPLICA | PUBSUB] [ID <client-id:count> ...]`
- Valkey: `[TYPE NORMAL | MASTER | REPLICA | PUBSUB] [ID <client-id:count> ...] [USER <username>] [ADDR <addr>] [LADDR <laddr>] [SKIPME YES | NO] [MAXAGE <maxage:count>]`
- Valkey-only keywords: `ADDR`, `LADDR`, `MAXAGE`, `NO`, `SKIPME`, `USER`, `YES`

### `CLUSTER|SETSLOT`

- Redis:  `<slot:count> IMPORTING <importing> | MIGRATING <migrating> | NODE <node> | STABLE`
- Valkey: `<slot:count> IMPORTING <importing> | MIGRATING <migrating> | NODE <node> | STABLE [TIMEOUT <timeout:seconds>]`
- Valkey-only keywords: `TIMEOUT`

### `SET`

- Redis:  `<key:key> <value> [NX | XX] [GET] [EX <seconds:seconds> | PX <milliseconds:milliseconds> | EXAT <unix-time-seconds:unix seconds> | PXAT <unix-time-milliseconds:unix milliseconds> | KEEPTTL]`
- Valkey: `<key:key> <value> [NX | XX | IFEQ <comparison-value>] [GET] [EX <seconds:seconds> | PX <milliseconds:milliseconds> | EXAT <unix-time-seconds:unix seconds> | PXAT <unix-time-milliseconds:unix milliseconds> | KEEPTTL]`
- Valkey-only keywords: `IFEQ`

### `SHUTDOWN`

- Redis:  `[NOSAVE | SAVE] [NOW] [FORCE] [ABORT]`
- Valkey: `[[NOSAVE | SAVE] [NOW] [FORCE] | ABORT]`

### `ZSCAN`

- Redis:  `<key:key> <cursor:cursor> [MATCH <pattern:pattern>] [COUNT <count:count>]`
- Valkey: `<key:key> <cursor:cursor> [MATCH <pattern:pattern>] [COUNT <count:count>] [NOSCORES]`
- Valkey-only keywords: `NOSCORES`
